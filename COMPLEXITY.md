# Complexity audit

DESIGN.md Part G, "Six standing rules", requires every algorithm to be of low complexity:
O(n log n) or better, O(n²) only with a recorded reason, nothing cubic or worse outside a
decision procedure that stops at a budget. This file lists every place an audit of
2026-10-30 found that breaks the rule, and the state of each. An item is closed when its
fix is in and a scaling test (size n against 8n, ratio at most 24) passes, or when the
reason it stays is recorded here.

Status: `open`, `fixed` (with the test that checks it), `kept` (with the reason).

## Kept

| Where | Why |
|---|---|
| Fourier–Motzkin pairs | an elimination runs only while it adds no terms to the rows (DESIGN L1.38), so the pairs of one elimination are at most the rows it removes times the rows it adds back, and the rows never grow; a system needing a growing elimination is refused (Growth) |
| undeclared parameters (check), `apply` | each body checked once; a class is merged or loses a carrier at most (values + 6 × classes) times, each step O(log N) by union by rank, so O(N log N); an application over unions keeps one state per set of arrows still alive, at most 2^(A+1) for an operator of A arrows, a constant of the prelude, so it is linear in the parts of its arguments. The search this replaced (RUNS, CONTEXTS, COMBOS, F6, F18) was exponential and capped |
